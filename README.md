## Method

Figures are Market-Reports.com estimates of global market revenue in US dollars, base year 2025, forecasts to 2035. Each market is sized on its own definition. **Markets overlap** (an AI server is also part of AI infrastructure), so rows must not be added together. Detailed segment, regional, country and company data for every market are in the full reports at the source URLs.
